The WhatsApp assistant for financial advisors

Clients ask at night. The answer is already in Luca.

Beyanno answers your clients' recurring WhatsApp questions (VAT due, filing status, deadlines) automatically, by reading your Luca records. Replies go out within seconds from your own WhatsApp number, day or night.

  • Read-only Luca user: Beyanno never changes records, approves filings or makes payments.
  • Your own WhatsApp, linked by QR code. Clients keep writing to the number they already know.
  • No invented figures: if a record is missing, Beyanno says so and forwards the question to you.
  • Exceptional topics you define (e.g. tax enforcement notices) are forwarded to you.
  • Explains tax topics (VAT, withholding, provisional tax, social security) with examples and a source line.
